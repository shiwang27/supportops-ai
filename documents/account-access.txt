Account access

If a customer cannot sign in, first ask them to check that they are using the email address connected to their account.

If the password was forgotten, the customer can use the password reset link on the sign-in page. The reset email may take a few minutes to arrive. They should also check their spam or junk folder.

If the reset link has expired, the customer should request a new link. Reset links can only be used once.

If the customer still cannot sign in after resetting the password, collect the account email and the exact error message. Do not ask the customer to share their password.
